# Hacker CFP Tracker

A chronological list of open and upcoming hacker and information-security conference CFPs.

**Last checked: October 19, 2026.**

To contribute, add or update a conference with its name, conference dates, CFP dates, what organizers are looking for, and a submission link. Use an organizer page or the organizer's submission platform as the source.

Open CFPs are ordered by final submission deadline, upcoming CFPs by opening date, and undated CFPs by conference date. Times use the published deadline timezone; an omitted time or timezone means it was not verified. Main conference dates are listed, with training or workshop days noted where relevant. Soft deadlines are distinguished from final deadlines.

## Verification Notes

- **Scope:** This tracker emphasizes practitioner talks, hacker-community conferences, workshops, and related participatory sessions. Conference dates alone do not establish that a CFP is open; no annual opening dates have been inferred from previous years.
- **SANS extensions:** The individual [OSINT](https://www.sans.org/cyber-security-training-events/sans-osint-summit-2027) and [CTI](https://www.sans.org/cyber-security-training-events/cyber-threat-intelligence-summit-2027) pages explicitly extend their deadlines to October 26 at 5:00 PM ET. The general speaker-information page still lists October 19.
- **BSidesSF:** November 9 is a soft deadline. The [submission platform](https://pretalx.com/bsidessf-2027/) gives November 13 as the final deadline.
- **TROOPERS timezone:** The submission platform uses `Europe/Berlin`. This avoids relying on its prose label “CET” for a March 31 deadline that falls during daylight saving time.

[^sandiego]: The [Sessionize page](https://sessionize.com/bsides-san-diego-2027/) is configured to close December 20, 2026 at 11:59 PM PST, while its prose says 5:00 PM and contains a conflicting year. Treat **December 20, 2026 at 5:00 PM PST** as a conservative submission target and confirm the discrepancy with the organizers.

[^groningen]: The [submission platform](https://sessionize.com/bsides-groningen-2027) closes January 31, 2027 at 11:59 PM CET. The [conference website](https://bsidesgrunn.org/2025/01/21/cfp/) lists February 1 and also retains older review-process dates. This tracker uses the earlier submission-platform deadline.

[^wwhf]: The [organizer's event page](https://wildwesthackinfest.com/wild-west-hackin-fest-mile-high-2027/) currently links separate paper, workshop, and village forms. The paper form could not be read during this check. The previous tracker and [CFPTime](https://www.cfptime.org/) list October 30, 2026, but this has **not been reconfirmed from the organizer's form**. Verify it through the organizer's CFP link before relying on it.

[^identiverse]: The [organizer's homepage](https://identiverse.com/) advertises an open 2027 Call for Presentations and links to its Sessionboard portal. The portal could not be read during this check, so a closing date and final submission requirements remain unverified.
